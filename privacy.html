塔羅抽牌 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-10-01

塔羅抽牌是完全離線的 app。你的抽牌紀錄只儲存在你的裝置上。

  • 不收集任何個人資料，不上傳任何資料到伺服器；app 沒有網路權限。
  • 不使用分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。
  • 加速度感應器只在抽牌畫面用來判斷手機是否蓋下或掀開，讀數不會儲存或傳送。
  • 震動權限只用於蓋牌時的觸覺回饋。
  • 解除安裝 app 即刪除所有資料。

Privacy Policy

Tarot Draw (塔羅抽牌) is a fully offline app. Your reading history is stored only on your device.

  • No personal data is collected and nothing is uploaded to any server; the app has no internet permission.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • The accelerometer is used only on the drawing screen to detect whether the phone is placed face down or lifted; readings are never stored or transmitted.
  • The vibration permission is used only for haptic feedback when the phone is placed face down.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data.
