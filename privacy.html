塔羅抽牌 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-10-08

你的抽牌紀錄只儲存在你的裝置上，不會上傳到任何伺服器（我們沒有自己的伺服器）。

廣告

  • 沒有購買「移除廣告」時，抽完牌陣按「完成」後會顯示一則由 Google AdMob 提供的全螢幕廣告。
  • AdMob 會使用你的裝置廣告 ID、IP 位址（用來推估大概位置）與裝置資訊，來提供與評估廣告、防止詐騙。 詳見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料。
  • 你可以在 Android 的「設定 › 隱私權 › 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID。
  • 歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者，在第一次載入廣告前會被詢問是否同意；之後可以在 app 首頁右上角「⋮ › 廣告隱私設定」更改。
  • 買過「移除廣告」之後，app 不會再載入任何廣告。

「移除廣告」購買

  • 一次性購買，透過 Google Play 付款；付款資料由 Google 處理，我們拿不到。
  • 購買紀錄綁定你的 Google 帳號，換手機或重新安裝時，用同一個帳號就能在「⋮ › 還原購買」還原。
  • 退款依照 Google Play 的退款政策處理。

其他權限

  • 加速度感應器只在抽牌畫面用來判斷手機是否蓋下或掀開，讀數不會儲存或傳送。
  • 震動權限只用於蓋牌時的觸覺回饋。
  • 網路權限只用於載入廣告、以及跟 Google Play 確認購買。
  • 解除安裝 app 即刪除裝置上的所有資料；購買紀錄保留在你的 Google Play 帳號裡。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

Your reading history is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server (we don't run one).

Ads

  • Unless you have bought "Remove ads", a full-screen ad provided by Google AdMob is shown after you tap "Done" at the end of a reading.
  • AdMob uses your device's advertising ID, IP address (to estimate approximate location) and device information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before ads are first loaded, and can change it later under ⋮ › Ad privacy settings on the home screen.
  • After you buy "Remove ads", the app no longer loads any ads.

"Remove ads" purchase

  • A one-time purchase paid through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; we never receive them.
  • The purchase is tied to your Google account. On a new phone or after reinstalling, use ⋮ › Restore purchase with the same account.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Other permissions

  • The accelerometer is used only on the drawing screen to detect whether the phone is placed face down or lifted; readings are never stored or transmitted.
  • The vibration permission is used only for haptic feedback when the phone is placed face down.
  • The internet permission is used only to load ads and to check purchases with Google Play.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data on your device; your purchase stays with your Google Play account.
